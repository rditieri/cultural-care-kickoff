How I want you to engage:
- When I ask you to argue with me: do it fully. Don't hedge.
- When I ask for ideas: give me volume first, quality second.
- When I ask what I'm missing: be honest, even if it's uncomfortable.
- When I share a plan: assume I want critique, not validation, unless I say otherwise.

Build the doc in three steps:
1. Write it up under clear headings. Keep my words & specifics. Don't invent facts, names or numbers about me or my team.
2. Add a section called "How to help me best": based on what I told you, what help will matter most to me, what to push me on & what to skip. If you have the Cultural Care Context, add a line on how my work connects to our goals. Mark anything you're inferring as "(my read)".
3. Ask me 3 questions whose answers would make this doc much more useful. When I answer, rewrite the full doc with my answers folded in, ready to save.

The breakout, on paper

If the tech fails, run it as a group conversation.

  1. Pick one team's commitment from this morning.
  2. Pre-mortem it: it's a year from now and it failed. Why?
  3. Flip it: what would have to be true? What aren't we doing yet?
  4. Come up with 30 ideas, not 5. Pick the best two.
  5. Write down the one thing you'd do differently & the strongest objection to it.
